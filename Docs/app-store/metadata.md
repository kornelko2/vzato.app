**Name:** Vzato – Medication Reminder
**Subtitle:** Never wonder "did I take it?"

**Promotional text**
Reminds you of every dose, shows what you already took and keeps an eye on your supply. No account, no ads: your data stays on your devices and in your iCloud.

**Keywords**
pill,reminder,medication,tracker,dose,tablet,organizer,refill,medicine,drops,health,senior

**Description**
Vzato reminds you of every medication at the right time and, above all, tells you whether you already took it. No more "did I take it this morning?".

• One big card for the dose that is due, with a big Taken button
• Reminders with Taken, Snooze and Skip, repeated when you don't confirm
• Tablets, half and quarter tablets, drops, millilitres, teaspoons, puffs
• History with a calendar, corrections, export for your doctor (CSV)
• Supply tracking: counts down by itself, warns when you run low and estimates when you run out
• Supply check: count the pills at home and Vzato shows whether the records match
• Photos of the package, the pill and the leaflet
• Home screen and Lock Screen widgets with a Taken button
• Sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own iCloud
• Activity log: who recorded a dose, when and on which device
• Czech, Slovak, English and Hungarian

Privacy first: no account, no server, no analytics, no ads. The developer never sees your data.

Vzato is free and open source (MPL-2.0).

Vzato is not a medical device and does not replace your doctor or pharmacist. It gives no medical advice; it reminds you and keeps your own records.

## CloudKit container: deploy the schema to Production

Needed once before the first TestFlight build, and again after every model change.

1. Open https://icloud.developer.apple.com/ and sign in with the developer account.
2. CloudKit Database › choose the container **iCloud.app.vzato**.
3. Make sure the environment switch (top left) shows **Development** and that the record types
   `CD_Medication`, `CD_DoseEvent`, `CD_Schedule`, `CD_Profile`, `CD_MedicationPhoto` and
   `CD_ActivityEntry` exist under Schema › Record Types. If `CD_ActivityEntry` is missing, run a debug
   build on a signed-in device once (or with the launch argument `--init-cloudkit-schema`), then reload.
4. In the left column at the bottom click **Deploy Schema Changes…**, review the list, confirm **Deploy**.
5. Switch the environment to **Production** and check that the same record types are listed.

Nothing else is needed: Core Data creates the private database zone on each user's device.
